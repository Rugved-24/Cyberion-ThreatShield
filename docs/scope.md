# Scope and Acceptance Criteria

Independent detection-engineering work in a simulated client scenario.

Required outcomes:
- At least 3 real telemetry source types, with provenance and data dictionaries.
- At least 15 tested Sigma detections, including 3 correlation detections.
- Every rule includes ATT&CK mapping, severity rationale, false-positive notes,
  and reproducible evidence from real telemetry.
- At least 20 techniques assessed across 4 tactics, with justified coverage
  status and supporting rule references.
- At least 2 hypothesis-driven hunts, including one targeting an existing
  coverage gap.
- At least 2 evidence-confirmed incident investigations with timelines,
  root-cause assessment, impact, and justified closure classifications.
- At least 3 written response playbooks covering investigation, containment,
  eradication, recovery, and stakeholder communications.
- At least 3 observed false-positive tuning cases with before/after evidence.
- IOC research, methodology, executive summary, and final presentation.
- Version-controlled deliverables reproducible by another analyst.

Excluded: custom applications, backend/database deployment, live SIEM
deployment, production-client access, and automated response execution.

Work proceeds on demand. Advance only after the current phase checks pass.
