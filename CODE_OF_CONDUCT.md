# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Participation in Cyberion ThreatShield should be respectful, constructive and
focused on improving evidence-based defensive security work.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and specific when reviewing work.
- Critique technical claims and evidence, not people.
- Acknowledge uncertainty and distinguish facts from assumptions.
- Protect private data and respect third-party licensing.
- Keep discussion focused on defensive, authorized research.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, personal attacks, deliberate disclosure of private information, or
knowingly presenting unsupported security claims as proven results are not
acceptable.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may edit, reject or remove contributions that violate
these expectations. Use the contact method on the maintainer's GitHub profile to
report concerns.
